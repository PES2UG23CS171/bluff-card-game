♠ Bluff ♥

By Dhrushaj Achar

Lie about your cards. Call out your friends. Empty your hand first.

or join one

How to play
  • On your turn, put any number of cards face down in the pot and announce a rank, whether or not you really hold it.
  • Until the next player acts, anyone still holding cards can call bluff. The play is revealed: if it was honest the caller takes the whole pot, otherwise the liar does.
  • You may pass instead of playing. When everyone else has passed, the pot is set aside and the last player to play opens a new round.
  • The first player to get rid of all their cards wins. Keep going until one loser is left, or vote to restart.
